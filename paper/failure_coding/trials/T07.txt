## 2. Why 1 lamp and not 9

Lamp 1 was counted. Lamps 2-9 were never built, because there was no second light source.
- Seb's choices (not simulator defects):
  - Thursday went on cheap improvised bodies (bottle, ruler, pipe, hanger, card) and on a 12V route that needs a
    technician. The ready 131 stand was refused at first.
  - Seb never searched Manial for USB strips. Abdelrahman started there Sunday. The facts put 6 white USB strips
    (98 x2, 155 x4) and 2 folding stands (126) in two Manial accessory shops.
  - The Carrefour strip question came at 14:57 Sunday. Mostafa had stood in that mall on Thursday 15:57-16:35.
  - On Sunday Seb required one technician to measure, join, insulate and test, all within 15 minutes. That ruled
    out partial offers.
  - On Thursday Seb did not pair the Abbasiya electrician's 39 EGP join (free after 17:12, keeps work for pickup)
    with modules from another counter.
- World friction within the record's rates: Fares' misleading phone claim (about 16 min), Most's storefront not
  found (drawn worker mistake), Abdelrahman's Thursday reroute home (drawn worker mistake), no table at Carrefour
  (facts' 77-min cafe search), Lampatronics without signal.
- Simulator choice that may have cost lamps: Harby Tech never surfaced (H1). With it, up to 4 heads for the 4 stands
  were possible from about 15:00.

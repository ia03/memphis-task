## 2. Why 0 and not 9

Lamp 9: no light source.
- Seb found and bought 8 strips: all 6 at Lampatronics and both at downtown mobile shop 1.
- More existed in the facts: Abbasiya electronics shop 1 (5 at 146), Abbasiya mobile shop 1 (2 at 137), Noon (20 at
  104) and Jumia (20 at 151) online. Harby could have joined up to 11 12V module heads a day. Seb pursued none of these
  after Thursday 16:38. Cause: Seb.

Lamps 1-8: built, delivered on time, rejected for height.
- All 8 are one object each, stable, safe, delivered at 16:37. Three passed reading and writing under power.
- The only failing test was "about 20 cm or more". The units sit at about 18 cm.
- Causes, in order:
  1. Seb's own 13:28 instruction set the range at 15-20 cm. On Thursday he had asked for 20-35 cm. The same folding
     holder stands at about 22-30 cm in other runs of this seed (this round-s12-hinted, this round-s12-hinted).
  2. The simulator placed the head at 18 cm within that range, knowing the line (H1, doubt).
  3. The grader read "about 20" strictly (G1, doubt).
  4. Seb could not see the line. The task rules have no number. At 14:15 he wrote that "height of 15 cm is not a task
     requirement" (V2, design).
- Time and money were not limits. The units reached the door 23 minutes early. 3,373 EGP was unspent.

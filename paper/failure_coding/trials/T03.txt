## 2. Why 0 lamps (each lamp short of 9)

Lamp 1, the delivered unit, fails on height.
- Seb's own 15:15 geometry sets the roof at 18 cm.
- With two A3 sheets and a 21 cm A4 width, height plus width is at most 40 cm, so the height cannot pass 19 cm. Seb had
  four sheets at home (one creased). Three sheets joined would have allowed 22 wide x 20-25 high. B's 100x70 Canson was
  another option. Both were Seb's to choose.
- The facts mark `card-a3-300g-loose` height_ok false. The simulator labelled the unit `low` and the grader followed.

Lamps 2-9 never had a light source.
- The only ready light Seb found was Carrefour's single strip (count 1).
- Seb's choices: no Noon order. No search of Manial, B's Sunday start, where the facts hold 6 strips (98 and 155 EGP)
  and 2 folding stands (126). No Sunday visit to Lampatronics after he knew its address. 30 minutes on IKEA Cairo
  Festival City.
- World side: no joiner was ever reached for the 4 modules. Harby, Dokki electrician 1 (offered true, B's Thursday
  start district) and Hadayek October electrician 1 all do joins. Seb never asked for an electrician in Dokki or
  Hadayek. In Bab El Louk the simulator never surfaced Harby (H1).

Bodies: Seb bought no body with height_ok true. He turned down 4 stands at 131 twice. New Maadi shop 2 (4 at 175) was
visited on Sunday but not offered to him. Abdelrahman (Dokki, Thursday) was never sent to look at stands.

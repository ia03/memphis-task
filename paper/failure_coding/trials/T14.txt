## 2. Why all 9 lamps are missing


All 9 have one cause: no light source was ever joined to a power path.

Seb's light route: a 12V module (joining_required: true, bare leads) plus a female jack (joining_required: true,
  bare leads) plus a boost cable. That is a technician's join on every head. The workers may not wire (BRIEF). Mustafa
  said so at 16:03: "I can't connect wires".

Thursday's chance was the Bab El Louk electrician at 16:28. Seb refused it (no receipt, no test, work after the shift).
  The electrician's facts are receipt: false and tests_heads: false. Nothing was made up.

Sunday: Seb kept Mustafa in New Maadi (no joiner there), then sent him toward IKEA Cairo Festival City (absent), then
  to Carrefour (no USB light). He never sent anyone back to Bab El Louk. The known electrician, Harby (next to
  Lampatronics, 39 a head, 11 min, free on arrival), RAM (jacks at 6) and Lampatronics (6 white USB strips at 90) are
  all there.

Ready USB strips in the facts: Lampatronics 6 at 90; Bab El Louk mobile-accessories 1, 2 at 90; Abbasiya, 5 at 146
  and 2 at 137; IKEA (6th October) colour-changing at 389; Jumia and Noon online with 3-day delivery. The only one of
  these shops a worker entered was mobile-accessories 1, and there only to ask about fitting.

Holders: only one was made, a dummy-tested ruler triangle. More needed stationery; New Maadi has none.

Money was never a limit: 4,795 unspent.

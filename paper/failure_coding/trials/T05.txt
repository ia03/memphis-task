## 2. Why 1 lamp and not 9


Lamp 1 (A001): counted.

Lamps 2-4 (A002, A003, B001): built and delivered, but on low fixed stands. Cause: Seb's choice. He accepted 10-15 cm
  stands on Saturday, was told on Sunday they were low and fixed, and approved them. Nothing in the world forced this.
  Tall holders were in the facts elsewhere: New Maadi shop 1 had one left at 189 (Seb knew), New Maadi shop 2 had two at
  136, Dokki had them at 126 and 180, downtown accessories shop 1 had three at 204. On Sunday Seb did not have Mustafa
  buy shop 1's last holder before leaving New Maadi, and sent no one to look for tall stands. His only nudge was at the
  Abbasiya shop (14:23:56: report a pricier suitable stand if there is one), which had none.

Lamps 5-9: no heads. Harby can make 8 a day at 12 min each. Seb had parts for 7 (6 modules plus the prototype). Seb's
  cutoffs let Harby start only one head at 15:02, then nothing until 15:34. Seb admitted the cutoffs cost time. Even
  with more heads, there were no tall stands for them.

No lamp was lost to a world event. The only physical friction on the lamp path was the 2 min charger mistake and the
  6 min late stand report.

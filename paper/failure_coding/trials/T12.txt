## 2. Why 4 and not 9

Lamps 1-4: counted.
- Each was built from a Harby-joined head and a folding stand, tested on USB and handed over at 16:59:32.
- They were counted because the last 9 minutes went quickly: 2 minutes to unload, a 5-minute walk, 30 seconds at the
  door. A 7-minute walk would have lost all four (E1).

Lamps 5-9: never built. Causes, all Seb's:
- Supports. Seb owned 4 stands. The world offered more: Dokki mobile shop 1 (2 at 126, rejected Thursday 14:35),
  Thabet in Dokki (5 at 180), Manial mobile shop 2 (4 at 198, where B started Sunday), downtown mobile shop 1 (3 at
  204, about 6 minutes from Harby), Wasla online (16 at 50). On Sunday Seb searched New Maadi, plus one 5-minute
  ask near Harby.
- Heads. Harby could join 8 a day and keeps finished work for later pickup. Seb made no sample on Thursday and would
  not leave parts there without a receipt. On Sunday he ordered 4 at 14:08-14:12. A downtown electrician (75 a head,
  10 a day) was never found.
- Time. The route through Algeria Square and Mostafa's home took from 15:00 to 16:35. Seb chose a 10-minute first
  test, booked the car only after it, and tested one lamp at a time on one charger. Chargers were on sale at both New
  Maadi mobile shops.

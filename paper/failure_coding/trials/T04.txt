## 2. Why 4 and not 9

Each lamp needed one light source and one stand. Seb had four light sources. That set the count.
- Lamps 1-4: built and delivered (section 3).
- Lamps 5-9 (all five short): no fifth ready light source.
  - Every shop Seb asked had the stock the facts give: Thabet 1 strip, Lampatronics 3, New Maadi shops none,
    Carrefour none.
  - Other strips were in the facts but only by walking: FEC Egypt (19 Youssef El-Gendy, the same street as
    Lampatronics) 4 at 139, El Gammal (Bab El Louk) 4 at 159, two downtown walk-ins 1 each, Abbasiya 4, Maadi Degla 2.
    Mustafa was downtown 16:16-17:00 Thursday but was sent only to Lampatronics and Harby. Seb asked Lampatronics for
    more strips but never asked a worker to walk the street. Mustafa's facts give `relays_shop_alternatives` 0.15.
    The shop not pointing him next door is ordinary.
  - The 12V module route (Harby joins, boost cable) needed the parts left with Harby on Thursday. Seb bought them at
    16:59:40 with a strict 17:00 cutoff. On Sunday Seb chose not to go downtown. Seb also never learned that
    Lampatronics sells boost cables (facts: 20 at 50). Seb asked Harby, not Lampatronics, about a cable source.
  - Stands were a second limit beyond five: Thabet had 5. Other shops had the same stand at 168-216 (a Dokki walk-in,
    Manial, Maadi Degla, Abbasiya and others), found only by walking.
- Time was not the limit. All four were tested by 16:09. The worker mistakes cost about 17 min on the critical path
  (6 on the handoff, 11 on assembly). They cut the delivery margin, not the count.

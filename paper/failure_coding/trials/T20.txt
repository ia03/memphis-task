## 2. Why 3 lamps, not 9

Each lamp short of 9 is missing a structure. Nothing else ran out.
- Heads: 13 were prepared and power paths were proven. 10 went unused.
- Money: 2,574 EGP was unspent at 17:00.
- Time: delivery finished at 15:59, 61 minutes before the deadline.
- Structures: Seb bought 3. The facts held more valid ones. Seb knew about the first three groups below from worker
  reports on Thursday:
  - New Maadi mobile shop 1: 2 folding stands at 121, a 6-minute walk from the Sunday assembly desk.
  - Thabet, Dokki: 2 arms at 500 and 5 folding stands at 180.
  - Bab El Louk stationery: Canson 300 g sheets and binder clips for the card fold.
  - Not found by this run: Abbasiya (4 arms, 6 folding stands), Hadayek October (2 arms, 1 folding stand), Sixth
    of October (2 folding stands).
- Seb's Sunday plan for extra supports was a bottle, a ruler, a box, or more low cradles. The facts say none of
  these can hold a light over a desk. These were Seb's choices, and the world gave the results the facts require.

So lamps 4 to 9 are all lost to Seb's decisions. None is lost to the simulator.

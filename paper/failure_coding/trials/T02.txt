## 2. Why each lamp is short of 9

All nine are short for one reason. Seb never got a structure with `height_ok: true`.
- Every structure in facts that could pass sits in districts nobody visited: Dokki, Manial, Maadi Degla, Hadayek October, Abbasiya, 6th October. The rest are online with 7-8 days' delivery, or behind a phone nobody answers.
- b stood in Dokki on Thursday and in Manial on Sunday. Both have tall stands (Thabet Group in Dokki; Manial accessory shops at 213 and 381 EGP). Seb routed him elsewhere both times without asking what was nearby. That was Seb's choice. Walk-in shops are only found by walking past them.

- The other 7 strips were never mounted, because time ran out. They would not have counted either.

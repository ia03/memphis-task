## 2. Why all 9 lamps are missing


Two units were delivered. Both fail for the same reason: the body. The other 7 were never built.

Cause 1, the body (Seb's design). The A3 tent gives no room to write under the light (section 3). Every unit on it
  fails. That alone fixes the score at 0.

Cause 2, light sources. Seb had 3 ready strips (all of Lampatronics' stock) plus one late joined module. He left
  Thabet's single strip (146) on Thursday. So at most 4 light sources existed. All would have gone into A3 tents.

Cause 3, time. Mustafa spent 13:00-16:10 on rides, a downtown paper purchase and a mall table search. Assembly began
  at 16:12, and the departure target was 16:25.

The simulator lost no lamp. The only simulator-side delay on the light path (H1) touched a module that would also
  have gone into a low tent.
